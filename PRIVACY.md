\# TableHighlighter — Privacy Policy



\*\*Last updated:\*\* October 21, 2026



\## Overview



TableHighlighter is a Chrome browser extension designed to improve readability of HTML tables by highlighting the row and column of a selected cell.



Your privacy is important. TableHighlighter operates locally within your browser and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.



\## Information Accessed



TableHighlighter accesses HTML table structures on webpages solely to identify selected cells and apply visual highlighting.



All processing occurs locally in your browser. Webpage content is not recorded, transmitted, or stored.



\## Local Storage



TableHighlighter uses Chrome's local storage API to remember whether the highlighting feature is enabled or disabled.



This preference is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or third parties.



\## Data Collection and Sharing



TableHighlighter:



\- Does not collect personal information.

\- Does not track browsing history.

\- Does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

\- Does not transmit webpage content to external servers.

\- Does not sell or share user information with third parties.



\## Permissions



TableHighlighter requires access to websites to identify HTML table cells and visually highlight their corresponding rows and columns.



The Storage permission is used exclusively to save the user's highlighting preference.

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data-handling practices change.



Any updates will be published at this location.
